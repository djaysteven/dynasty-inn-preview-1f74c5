What is already there

  • The name Dynasty Inn Hotel, Pattaya.
  • One property photograph, used as the feature background.
  • The line “Currently Open!”
  • A short note about Pattaya Beach, reopening, and a special rate — with no rate figure.
  • Email didowntown@yahoo.com and a mail button.
  • Address 596/16 Soi 13, Pattaya Beach Road, Pattaya 20150.

What the template still shows

  • The placeholder line “My strapline goes here”.
  • A button that still reads “Lorem Ipsum”.
  • The Francesca template’s stock feature image, as well as the real photograph.
  • The BaseKit logo field is empty. The property photograph still carries the burgundy di monogram. No phone number is on the rendered page.

What this demo does

  • Shows that same photograph once, in a frame, with a light retouch only.
  • Uses the published di monogram — the same burgundy circle and white letters — in the header and the favicon, with the name beside it.
  • Keeps the note, the open line, the email, and the address — in English and Thai.
  • Drops the placeholder strapline, the lorem button, and the stock background.
  • Does not add rooms, prices, amenities, testimonials, or a booking form.

Demo by Vantage. The inn’s words and photograph stay theirs.
